About Founder

Vinamrata Giri

Founder of Dirghayuyog, located in Kalyan, Mumbai.

She has completed her MA in Yograshtra and has more than 10 years of teaching experience in Yoga and wellness training.

During her college years, she achieved excellence by winning Gold and Silver medals in State Level Yoga Competitions.

Her mission is to help people achieve a healthy lifestyle through traditional yoga, meditation, breathing practices, and mental wellness techniques.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What's the age limit for yoga?
Yoga is for all ages! From kids to seniors, everyone can benefit. We have classes tailored for different age groups and fitness levels.
❓ Do I need equipment?
No! All equipment like yoga mats, blocks, and belts are provided by us. Just bring yourself and wear comfortable clothes.
❓ What if I'm not flexible?
Perfect! That's exactly why you need yoga. Flexibility comes with practice. Our beginner classes are designed for people starting from zero flexibility.
❓ How long to see results?
Most students notice improvements in 2-4 weeks. For weight loss, it typically takes 8-12 weeks with consistent practice and healthy diet.
❓ Can I join anytime?
Yes! You can join our classes anytime. We offer a free demo class so you can experience our teaching style first.
❓ Do you offer online classes?
Currently, we offer in-person classes in Kalyan. Contact us to inquire about upcoming online options.
❓ Is there a refund policy?
Yes! If you're not satisfied within 7 days, we offer a full refund. Your satisfaction is our priority.
❓ Can I bring a friend?
Absolutely! We have special discounts for group bookings. Contact us for group rates.
